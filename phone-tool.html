Replace Repo & Reverse

Upload a zip + repo URL → Replace backs up the current repo (to a hidden _backup_<branch> branch) and overwrites it with your zip's files. Changed your mind? ↩ Reverse restores the backed-up version. Uses the dashboard's GitHub token.
